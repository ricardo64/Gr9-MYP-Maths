1) 6x + 5 = 47

6x = 42 (−5 both sides)

x = 7 (÷6)

2) 15 = 29 − x

15 + x = 29 (+x both sides)

x = 14 (−15)

3) 8x + 4 = 5x + 22

3x + 4 = 22 → 3x = 18

x = 6 (÷3)

4) 7x − 5 = 10 − 3x

10x − 5 = 10 → 10x = 15

x = 1.5 (÷10)

Plenary • Problem 1

Comparing Rectangle Areas

Question d)

Rectangle X measures 7 cm by (x + 1) cm. Rectangle Y has dimensions (3x + 6) cm and 4 cm. The area of Rectangle X is half the area of Rectangle Y. Calculate the side lengths and areas.

Line-by-Line Working
2 × 7(x + 1) = 4(3x + 6)
14x + 14 = 12x + 24
2x = 10 → x = 5
Rect X: 7 cm × 6 cm = 42 cm²
Rect Y: 21 cm × 4 cm = 84 cm²

Plenary • Super Challenge Question

Isosceles Triangle Stick Lengths

Adam has sticks of lengths (3x + 4) cm, (2x + 10) cm, and (x + 12) cm to make an isosceles triangle. Calculate the 3 possible values of x.

Case 1: Stick 1 = Stick 2

3x + 4 = 2x + 10

x = 6

Case 2: Stick 1 = Stick 3

3x + 4 = x + 12

2x = 8 → x = 4

Case 3: Stick 2 = Stick 3

2x + 10 = x + 12

x = 2
